Good to know.

Can you upgrade any European car?

We assess each vehicle individually. Model year alone doesn’t establish compatibility; the screen, factory system, audio equipment and previous modifications all matter.

Will I keep my original controls and camera?

Yes. Keep your original controls, factory camera and access to your vehicle’s menus while adding Apple CarPlay and Android Auto. We match the hardware to your setup and test these functions during installation.

How much does it cost?

CarPlay adapter installations start at $350, and screen replacements start at $450. Final pricing depends on your vehicle’s year, model and configuration.

Where do you install?

Mobile installation is available throughout the DC metro area.
