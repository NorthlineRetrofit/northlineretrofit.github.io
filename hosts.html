How does CarPlay help my Turo listing?

Apple CarPlay and Android Auto give you practical features to promote alongside your car’s luxury appeal. Turo lets guests filter by these features, so an accurately tagged listing can match guests who specifically want that connectivity. Show the working system in your listing photos and describe how guests connect.

Will a retrofit automatically appear in Turo’s filters?

Check your listing after installation. Turo uses trim-based features and additional options; if the installed feature is missing, contact Turo support. A description or custom feature alone does not establish that the filter tag has been added. Confirm the tag before relying on filter visibility. Turo’s feature guidance ↗

Can you fit several cars in one visit?

We can discuss a shared visit after reviewing each vehicle. Timing depends on the hardware, work area, travel and installation complexity. The schedule is confirmed before booking.

Is this an official Turo service?

No. Northline Retrofit is an independent installer. Hosts are responsible for their vehicle’s platform eligibility and listing details.

What happens if guests have trouble connecting?

We walk through pairing and switching phones at handover. Ask us to confirm the support arrangements and hardware warranty in writing with your quote.
