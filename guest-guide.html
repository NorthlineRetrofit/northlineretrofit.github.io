YOUR CONNECTED DRIVE

Get connected.
Enjoy the drive.

Wireless Apple CarPlay on this BMW: connect your iPhone, hear your audio and switch back to iDrive.

Set up your phone while safely parked.

Host / vehicle ID[Add host name and vehicle identifier]

Pairing device nameUse the Bluetooth name shown on the car’s screen.

01 / SWITCH MODES

Press and hold MENU.

Hold down the MENU button to switch between iDrive and CarPlay mode.

Start in CarPlay mode before pairing your phone.

02 / PAIR YOUR IPHONE

Connect wirelessly.

On your iPhone, open Settings → Bluetooth. Select the Bluetooth device whose name appears on the car’s screen and pair with it.

Once paired, wireless CarPlay will start.

03 / AUDIO IN THE BMW

Select AUX in iDrive.

Hold MENU to return to iDrive. Select AUX as the car’s audio input source.

Hold MENU again to return to CarPlay mode.

04 / AUDIO ON YOUR PHONE

Select CarPlay output.

On your iPhone, select CarPlay as the audio output.

For sound through the car, both settings matter: BMW input = AUX and iPhone output = CarPlay.

NEED A HAND?

Your host is your first contact.

Park safely before troubleshooting. Contact your host through your booking’s messaging channel. Do not factory-reset the screen.

Connected but no sound? Check AUX in iDrive and CarPlay as the audio output on your iPhone.
