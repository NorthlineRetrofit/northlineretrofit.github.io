iPhone quick-start guide2009 BMW X5 · E70

Sample handout. Host details and the vehicle-specific tutorial are added before guest use.

Get connected.
Enjoy the drive.

Wireless Apple CarPlay: connect your iPhone, hear your audio and switch back to iDrive.

Set up your phone while safely parked.

Host / vehicle IDAdd your host name and vehicle identifier

Bluetooth device nameUse the name shown on the car’s screen.

1 / Switch modes

Press and hold MENU.

Hold the MENU button to switch between iDrive and CarPlay mode.

Start in CarPlay mode before pairing your phone.

2 / Pair your iPhone

Connect wirelessly.

Open Settings → Bluetooth on your iPhone. Pair with the Bluetooth device whose name appears on the car’s screen.

Once paired, wireless CarPlay will start.

3 / Audio in the BMW

Select AUX in iDrive.

Hold MENU to return to iDrive. Select AUX as the car’s audio input source.

Hold MENU again to return to CarPlay.

4 / Audio on your phone

Select CarPlay output.

On your iPhone, select CarPlay as the audio output.

For sound through the car, use both:
BMW input = AUX
iPhone output = CarPlay

Connected, but no sound?

Check AUX in iDrive and CarPlay as your iPhone’s audio output.

Need a hand? Contact your host.

Park safely and use your booking’s messaging channel. Do not factory-reset the screen.
