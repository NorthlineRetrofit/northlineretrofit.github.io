# BMW MMI enquiry catalogue

Research checked September 7, 2026. Scope: aftermarket interfaces that integrate with a BMW factory display and controls, not replacement Android screens, generic USB dongles or audio-only Bluetooth adapters.

## What this can—and cannot—confirm

The website offers 36 model/body-style choices and 50 platform screening rows. This is a broad discovery catalogue, **not an exhaustive, certified fitment database**. Northline’s box brand/SKU has not been supplied. There is no defensible universal yes/no result based on model and model year alone. The installer must confirm the actual head unit, screen/connector, audio route, build month, market, existing modifications and matching interface SKU before quoting.

The data deliberately separates customer-facing model names from platform research windows. Years are approximate enquiry windows, not an assertion that every listed chassis was produced or sold in every market throughout that interval. All years remain selectable, including those outside the research windows. Out-of-range and unknown selections go to individual review; they are not rejected as incompatible. Engine badges and M Sport appearance packages do not establish the infotainment hardware. The customer selects only the model family and year; exact variant and hardware details are confirmed privately with the installer.

The explicit check returns “Potentially compatible” only when a researched platform window matches without an extra review flag. All other cases return “Needs a manual check,” not “incompatible.” Both require a dashboard photo before confirmation. Technical platform codes and research references remain in a collapsed disclosure rather than the primary customer result.

## Sources and findings

- [Mr12Volt CCC/M-ASK interface](https://www.mr12volt.com/collections/bmw/products/p3000-bmcc): establishes older factory-screen routes for E9x, E6x, E70 and E71 platforms. Its MOST/audio and coding requirements are specific to that product; do not apply them to every MMI brand.
- [Mr12Volt CIC interface](https://www.mr12volt.com/collections/bmw/products/p3000-bmci): supports the principal CIC-era platform families, including E89 Z4, with screen/system requirements. Published year cutoffs are not universal exclusions for other suppliers.
- [Mr12Volt NBT/ID4 interface](https://www.mr12volt.com/collections/bmw/products/p3000-bmid): supports the principal F-platform NBT families and i8. The page’s 6.5-inch restriction depends on the head-unit manufacturer. Its claim of an E84 NBT fitment conflicts with other factory-system guidance; the catalogue does not infer factory NBT on every late E84.
- [Mr12Volt EVO interface](https://www.mr12volt.com/collections/bmw/products/p3000-bmev): lists F platforms and certain G-platform applications but excludes 6.5-inch displays for this product. A G05 or G02 listing cannot establish compatibility for all vehicles with that chassis code.
- [Mr12Volt i3 interface](https://www.mr12volt.com/collections/bmw/products/p3000-bmi3): its early i3 application requires the larger display. This is why i3 receives a screen-specific caution rather than a generic approval.
- [Road Top NBT/EVO interface](https://roadtop.com/products/bmw-nbt-evo-wireless-carplay): corroborates F3x, F07 and several X-family applications, including F85/F86. The page mixes broad marketing year ranges with narrower system exclusions. Its explicit CIC exclusion takes precedence over its generic promotional wording; years alone are not reliable.
- [BimmerTech professional head-unit guide](https://www.bimmer-tech.net/blog/item/54-bmw-cic-nbt-nbtevo): used for approximate system transitions and platform names, not an automatic VIN decoder. Its production-time table contains grouped chassis and historical “present” endpoints, so it is not an exact model-year production register. Newer/MGU transitions require individual review.
- [BimmerTech basic head-unit guide](https://www.bimmer-tech.net/blog/item/101-basic-bmw-navigation): demonstrates why small/basic screens cannot simply be called NBT or EVO based on the menu appearance. ENTRYNAV/ENAVEVO may create additional routes in selected cars, including some newer generations. These are conditional possibilities, not evidence that all G-platform cars accept an older MMI box.
- [BimmerTech MMI requirements](https://www.bimmer-tech.net/blog/item/49-how-to-retrofit-carplay): distinguishes interface retrofits from factory CarPlay activation and replacement head units. Its CHAMP2 support qualification is F3x-specific, so the website flags the entire basic-system category for exact hardware review.
- [Andream MMI catalogue](https://andream-eu.com/product/ewm-bmcp/): gives additional named M-platform leads, including F87/F80/F82/F83 and F90. Some stated years are inconsistent or overly broad; it is not used as a universal production calendar or a sole fitment authority.
- [Integrated Automotive 2 Series interface](https://integratedautomotive.co.uk/product/2014-2019-bmw-2-series-all-body-shapes-f22-f45-and-f46-carplay-and-android-auto-retrofit-multimedia-interface/): additional direct evidence for Active/Gran Tourer enquiry coverage, subject to supplier confirmation.

## Inclusion and uncertainty rules

1. `compatibility-data.js` is the editable source of truth. Every row includes its source IDs, possible system families and, where needed, an extra review flag.
2. A row with no flag still requires confirmation. It is not an approved Northline SKU mapping.
3. `mixed` marks generations where newer/native-connectivity configurations overlap the researched interface possibilities. These receive a manual-check result; the site does not claim an older-system box fits MGU/iDrive 7+. Hardware is confirmed from the subsequent enquiry, not a customer-facing technical dropdown.
4. `regional` includes platform-family extensions such as long-wheelbase or less-documented derivatives. `derivative` marks M variants inferred from a documented base platform where exact variant/year evidence is incomplete. Neither flag is independent confirmation of fitment. Some multi-code rows also group regional/body-style derivatives for enquiry routing; the individual code still needs checking.
5. `screen` adds an i3-specific display-size warning and a manual-check result. Basic systems always require exact identification. A missing factory screen or an aftermarket/swapped head unit invalidates any model/year suggestion and must be checked by the installer before confirmation.
6. Some choices intentionally have no candidate rows: 2 Series Gran Coupe, X7, M8, X3 M and X4 M. They are selectable for review, not silently labelled supported. “Other BMW / not sure” also covers older navigation architectures, newer electric models, imports and unlisted trims.
7. The 2008–2013 1 Series window does not claim that every older CCC-equipped 1 Series is excluded. Likewise, late Tourer years or a newer G-platform outside a screening window may have an option; the fallback keeps the enquiry open.
8. Photos should show the original BMW menu and screen. Ask for build month/VIN privately if the photo and vehicle details are insufficient. The site does not collect a VIN or upload photos.

## Follow-up to make this supplier-specific

Obtain the exact interface manufacturer/SKUs and installation instructions; map supported screens, wiring and head units per SKU; verify market/model-year boundaries and special variants; then have the installer approve the catalogue. Until then, retain the conditional wording. Do not promote this to a guaranteed or exhaustive compatibility list.
